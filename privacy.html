Last updated: [DATE]

Budgetarian ("the app") is a personal budgeting app operated by [YOUR FULL NAME] in the Philippines. This policy explains what the app collects and how it is used. Contact: [YOUR CONTACT EMAIL].

What we collect

  • Account information (Premium only): an account is optional. If you sign in for Premium, we store your email address and a password (stored securely and never visible to us), used only to sign you in.
  • Budget data you enter: payroll amounts, budget lines, spending, income, bank names and balances, and reconciliation items. You type these in yourself. The app does not connect to your bank and never asks for bank logins, card numbers or account numbers.

How it is used

Your data is used only to run the app for you: to save your budget and show it on any device where you sign in. We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties, except the service provider below that stores it.

Where it is stored

Without an account, your budget is stored only on your device. With Premium, your account and budget are also stored with Supabase (supabase.com), a cloud database provider, so they sync across your devices. Database rules ensure each account can only read its own data.

Your rights

Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012, you can access, correct, export and delete your data. In the app you can export your data (Settings > Back up your data) and permanently delete your account and all saved budgets (Settings > Account > Delete my account). You can also email [YOUR CONTACT EMAIL] for help.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). No method of storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Children

The app is not intended for children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
